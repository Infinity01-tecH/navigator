Trust block

Real content, never hallucinated.

After watching a roadmap get generated, the obvious question is: "Wait, are those links even real?" Here is the hard rule that makes the answer yes.

  • 1 No fake links. The generator is not allowed to invent URLs, resources, or citations. If a video, article, or tool cannot be verified, it is not included.
  • 2 Every resource resolves. Every item that ships in a roadmap either points to a real, reachable source or is removed before it ever reaches you.
  • 3 This is a mechanism, not a claim. The verification step runs automatically. It is the reason you can trust what you are clicking, not a marketing line.

If a link is broken or a source disappears, the roadmap flags it. You never have to wonder whether what you are learning from actually exists.

01

Full Roadmap

The complete, personalized curriculum — phases, steps, real resources, milestones, and a projected finish date. For when you want the whole path mapped out, not just the next thing to watch.

What you get

Every phase breaks down into Watch / Read / Build / Practice steps, each pointing to a real, verified resource — no invented links. Pass a quick check to unlock the next one, and watch your finish date sharpen as you go.

02

Sprint Blueprint

A focused 1–4 week plan built around one goal. No filler, no detours — just what it takes to ship the thing you're actually trying to build.

What you get

A single outcome, broken into a day-by-day task list with a countdown to your deadline. Ideal for a portfolio piece, an interview prep push, or any skill you need fast.

03

YouTube-Based

Built around creators you already trust. Navigator structures their videos into an actual path instead of leaving you to figure out the order yourself.

What you get

Pick the channels you already learn from, and Navigator sequences their videos into phases and steps — same structure as a Full Roadmap, built entirely from creators you chose.

Meet Navi

Stuck isn't a stopping
point — it's a question away.

Navi is the AI tutor built into Navigator. It already knows what you're learning right now, so answers land in context instead of a pile of generic search results.

  • 1 Ask by typing or voice. Get unstuck mid-step without breaking your flow to go dig through a forum.
  • 2 Keep a conversation per topic. Navi tracks context across multiple threads, so you never have to re-explain where you are.
  • 3 Save any answer to your notes. One tap sends it straight to the Notes Hub, tied to the topic it belongs to — nothing useful gets lost in a chat scrollback.

It's the difference between googling it and having someone who already knows your roadmap explain it.

Immersive Mode

That YouTube video isn't
a video anymore — it's a lesson.

Paste any YouTube link and Navigator turns it into a guided, chaptered lesson — the same structure as a roadmap step, built from a video you already wanted to watch.

  • 1 AI-generated chapters. Long videos get broken into named sections, so you always know what part you're in and what's next.
  • 2 Knowledge checks along the way. Short quizzes drop in at chapter boundaries — watching turns into proving you actually got it.
  • 3 Capture notes as you watch. Jot a thought mid-video and it's saved to the right topic in your Notes Hub automatically — and it still counts toward XP.

No sign-up required to try it — paste a link and see your first chaptered lesson before you commit to anything.

Accountability & Social

Motivation runs out.
Accountability doesn't.

Add streak partners who see your daily plan, cheer the wins, and notice when you go quiet — the same reason a gym buddy works better than a New Year's resolution.

  • 1 Streak partners. Pair up with a friend and see each other's daily progress side by side — not just a number, the actual plan.
  • 2 Reactions, not reports. A quick fire, clap, or nudge on a friend's step — lightweight enough to send every day.
  • 3 Friend requests built in. Find people already learning what you're learning, or bring your own.

You can learn alone. You're just less likely to finish that way.
